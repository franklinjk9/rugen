How It Works

Generate a custom `.ovpn` file to use RiseupVPN with any standard OpenVPN client.

Configure Your Connection

First, customize the VPN configuration to suit your needs on the main page.

  • Protocol: Choose UDP for faster speeds (great for streaming) or TCP for better reliability on unstable networks.
  • Locations: Select one or more server locations you'd like to use.
  • Advanced Options: It's highly recommended to keep Prevent DNS Leaks enabled to protect your privacy.

Generate and Download

Once you've made your selections, complete the CAPTCHA and click the "Generate & Download" button to get your custom .ovpn configuration file.

Import and Connect

To use the file, you need an OpenVPN client. If you don't have one, download the right client for your platform from the list below. Then, simply import the .ovpn file into the client and click connect.
